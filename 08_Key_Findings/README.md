# Key Findings & Business Insights

## 1. Executive Summary

The Product Portfolio & Investment Analysis project evaluates 20 products of Nova Consumer Products Ltd. across five years, from 2021 to 2025.

The analysis covers sales performance, profitability, market growth, relative market share, and BCG Matrix classification using MySQL.

The business generated total revenue of ₹52.71 crore and gross profit of ₹17.31 crore during the five-year period. Its overall gross profit margin was approximately 32.83%.

The BCG Matrix classified the 20 products into four categories: 6 Stars, 6 Cash Cows, 4 Question Marks, and 4 Dogs.

## 2. Sales Performance Findings

- Total revenue increased from ₹7.92 crore in 2021 to ₹13.51 crore in 2025.
- Revenue recorded positive year-on-year growth throughout the five-year period.
- Revenue grew by 13.64% in 2025 compared with 2024.
- VitaBoost Juice generated the highest cumulative revenue of approximately ₹3.73 crore.
- EnergyRush Drink and ChocoBite Cookies were the second- and third-highest cumulative revenue contributors.
- EnergyPlus Cereal recorded the highest 2025 year-on-year product revenue growth of 36%, followed by EnergyRush Drink at 31.25%.

**Business Insight:** The company demonstrated consistent revenue expansion, while selected products showed particularly strong growth momentum.

## 3. Profitability Findings

- Total gross profit during 2021–2025 was approximately ₹17.31 crore.
- The overall gross profit margin was approximately 32.83%.
- VitaBoost Juice contributed the highest share of total gross profit at 7.53%.
- EnergyRush Drink contributed 7.49%, followed by ChocoBite Cookies at 6.57%.
- FreshGlow Soap, BabyFresh Wipes, and PureCare Face Wash recorded the highest gross profit margins of 35%.
- AquaPure Detergent, BabySoft Diapers, and GentleBaby Shampoo generated relatively high cumulative revenue but recorded gross margins of 30%, below the portfolio average.

**Business Insight:** Revenue generation and profitability do not always move together. Management should focus on cost efficiency and margin improvement for products generating substantial revenue but relatively lower gross margins.

## 4. Market Growth Findings

The 2025 market growth analysis identified the following category performance:

| Product Category | Market Growth |
|---|---:|
| Baby Care | 11.48% |
| Personal Care | 10.22% |
| Food | 9.54% |
| Home Care | 7.11% |
| Beverages | 5.42% |

Key observations:

- Baby Care recorded the highest market growth at 11.48%.
- Personal Care followed with 10.22% growth.
- EnergyRush Drink recorded the highest individual product market growth at 22.45%.
- BabyFresh Wipes and EnergyPlus Cereal also demonstrated strong market growth.
- CoolFizz Soda was the only product with a declining market, registering -1.03% growth.

**Business Insight:** Baby Care and Personal Care represent attractive market-growth opportunities, while products operating in slower-growing markets may require more selective investment strategies.

## 5. Relative Market Share Findings

The 2025 relative market share analysis identified the following competitive positions:

- BabySoft Diapers recorded the highest relative market share of 1.80.
- FreshGlow Soap followed at 1.75.
- NutriCrunch Biscuits recorded 1.73.
- Six products had relative market share below 1, indicating that their market share was lower than that of their largest competitor.

**Business Insight:** Products with relative market share above 1 demonstrate stronger competitive positions against their largest competitors. Products below 1 may require competitive positioning and market-share improvement strategies.

## 6. BCG Matrix Findings

Based on 2025 market growth compared with 2024 and 2025 relative market share, the product portfolio was classified as follows:

| BCG Category | Product Count | Portfolio Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Stars | 6 | 30% |
| Cash Cows | 6 | 30% |
| Question Marks | 4 | 20% |
| Dogs | 4 | 20% |
| **Total** | **20** | **100%** |

### Stars
EnergyRush Drink, BabyFresh Wipes, EnergyPlus Cereal, GentleBaby Shampoo, PureCare Face Wash, and ChocoBite Cookies.

These products operate in high-growth markets and have relative market share of at least 1. Management should consider supporting their growth through selective marketing, distribution, and product development investment.

### Cash Cows
FreshGlow Soap, HerbalFresh Toothpaste, NutriCrunch Biscuits, BabySoft Diapers, AquaPure Detergent, and GreenLeaf Tea.

These products have strong relative market share in lower-growth markets. Management should focus on maintaining profitability, customer retention, and operational efficiency.

### Question Marks
FreshAir Room Spray, TinyCare Baby Lotion, UltraWash Liquid, and SilkTouch Shampoo.

These products operate in high-growth markets but have relative market share below 1. Selective investment and regular performance monitoring can help determine which products have the potential to become Stars.

### Dogs
GoldenHarvest Noodles, SparkClean Floor Cleaner, VitaBoost Juice, and CoolFizz Soda.

These products have low market growth and relative market share below 1. Management should review their profitability, market potential, and resource requirements before approving additional investment.

## 7. Overall Business Insights

1. The company achieved consistent revenue growth over the five-year analysis period.
2. VitaBoost Juice was the largest cumulative revenue generator and gross profit contributor.
3. EnergyRush Drink demonstrated strong revenue growth, market growth, and competitive positioning.
4. Baby Care was the fastest-growing product category.
5. The portfolio contains a balanced number of Stars and Cash Cows, each representing 30% of the products.
6. Four products fall into the Dogs category and may require strategic review.
7. Products with high revenue but lower gross margins present opportunities for cost optimization.

## 8. Strategic Implications

Based on the findings, management should:

- Prioritize promising Star products with strong financial performance.
- Maintain the profitability of Cash Cow products.
- Selectively invest in Question Marks based on market-share improvement potential.
- Review the financial viability of Dogs before committing further resources.
- Monitor gross profit margins alongside revenue growth.
- Align future investment decisions with market opportunities and product-level financial performance.

## 9. Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates how SQL and the BCG Matrix framework can be combined to evaluate financial performance, competitive positioning, market opportunities, and product portfolio composition.

The findings provide a structured basis for product-level strategic review and more informed resource allocation.

**Data Disclaimer:** Nova Consumer Products Ltd. is a fictional company, and all data used in this project is simulated for educational and portfolio demonstration purposes.

**Classification Assumptions:** High market growth is defined as 10% or above, and high relative market share is defined as 1 or above. Market growth compares 2025 with 2024, while relative market share uses 2025 data.
