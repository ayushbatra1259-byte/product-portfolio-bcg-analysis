# Project Conclusion

This project, **Product Portfolio and Investment Analysis Using the BCG Matrix**, evaluated the performance and market position of 20 products of Nova Consumer Products Ltd. using five years of simulated sales, profitability, market, and investment-related data.

The analysis provided insights into revenue growth, product contribution, gross profitability, market growth, and relative market share. Based on these parameters, the BCG Matrix classified the portfolio into six Stars, six Cash Cows, four Question Marks, and four Dogs.

The findings indicate that the company should prioritize growth opportunities in high-potential Star products, maintain the profitability of Cash Cows, and adopt selective investment strategies for Question Marks. Dog products require closer performance evaluation before making decisions about further investment or portfolio rationalization.

An important observation was that **VitaBoost Juice**, despite being classified as a Dog, generated the highest cumulative revenue and gross profit contribution in the portfolio. This highlights why investment decisions should not rely on the BCG Matrix alone. Revenue contribution, profitability, cash generation, investment efficiency, and future market potential must also be considered.

Overall, this project demonstrates how SQL-based analysis can convert raw business data into meaningful insights and support structured product portfolio and investment decisions. It also highlights the importance of combining strategic frameworks with financial performance analysis before allocating business resources.
